Welcome to HomeDrive

Your own Google Drive, on this computer. Your files stay here, and you can reach them from your other devices.

A new folder is fine. Anything already in it shows up in HomeDrive.

Make your account

You'll use this to sign in on your devices. You can add accounts for other people later.

Lowercase letters, numbers, dots or dashes.
At least 8 characters is strongly recommended, since HomeDrive can be reached from the internet.

Connect to Tailscale

Tailscale is what lets your other devices reach HomeDrive securely, from home or anywhere. It's built in; you only need a free Tailscale account.

Starting…
  1. Click Sign in to Tailscale. A page opens in your browser.
  2. Sign in, or make a free account (Google, Apple, Microsoft or GitHub all work).
  3. Come back here. This page moves on by itself.
Almost there: approve the new device “homedrive” in the Tailscale admin console.
